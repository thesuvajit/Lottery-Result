❓ Frequently Asked Questions — Lottery Sambad & Dear Lottery

When is the Lottery Sambad result declared today? ▾

Today's Lottery Sambad Result is declared three times daily: 1:00 PM (Dear Morning Draw), 6:00 PM (Dear Day Draw), and 8:00 PM (Dear Night Draw). Official gazette sheets and winning numbers are published live immediately after each official government draw.

How can I check today's 1st Prize winning ticket number? ▾

You can check today's 1st Prize ₹ 1 Crore winning number directly at the top of our website. Select 1 PM, 6 PM, or 8 PM button, or enter your ticket number in our Quick Ticket Number Checker for instant verification.

Where can I download the official Lottery Sambad PDF Result? ▾

Click on the PDF button or Download Image button located directly under any draw result to view and save the official high-resolution government gazette result sheet.

How do I check Lottery Sambad old results and yesterday's draws? ▾

Scroll to the Previous Day Results section to view complete gazette sheets for yesterday's 1 PM, 6 PM, and 8 PM draws. You can also use the By Date calendar picker in the top bar to inspect historical results for any selected date.
